# RetailPulse — Phase 2 Business Questions

Use the RFM dataset to answer:

1. Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
2. What percentage of customers are At Risk or Hibernating/Lost?
3. How much revenue is associated with high-value customers at risk?
4. Do Champions have materially higher AOV than other segments?
5. Which states contain the largest concentration of high-value customers?
6. What retention action should be recommended for each segment?

## Expected deliverable
A one-page analyst summary containing:
- 3 key findings
- 3 recommended actions
- 2 charts
- assumptions/limitations
