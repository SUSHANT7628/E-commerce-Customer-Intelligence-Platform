# RetailPulse Power BI Dashboard

Build a 4-page Power BI report from the final analytical model.

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPIs: Revenue, Orders, Customers, AOV, Revenue Growth.
Charts: monthly revenue, revenue by category, revenue by state.

## Page 2 — Customer Intelligence
KPIs: active customers, repeat rate, average CLV.
Charts: customer segments, revenue by segment, RFM distribution, CLV distribution.

## Page 3 — Churn & Retention
KPIs: churn rate, high-risk customers, high-value/high-risk customers.
Charts: CLV vs churn probability, retention priority distribution, high-risk customer table.

## Page 4 — Product & Seller
Charts: top categories, top products, seller revenue, review score vs revenue.

Every visual should answer a business question; avoid decorative charts.
